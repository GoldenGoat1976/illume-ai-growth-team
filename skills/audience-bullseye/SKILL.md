# Audience Bullseye
1. Select one service, customer decision, and local audience. Never infer patient diagnoses.
2. Map five rings: ready-to-book, symptom/problem-aware, solution-aware, adjacent wellness interest, broad lifestyle discovery.
3. For each ring specify desired outcome, language used, objection, ethical hook, and booking relevance. Treat assumptions as hypotheses.
4. Produce seven creative concepts: 3 core, 2 adjacent, 2 broad. Score 1-5 on resonance, novelty, local relevance, clinical accuracy, production ease.
5. Hand top concepts to Spark for scripts, Muse for brand review, Sentinel for clinical claims review, and Conductor for human publishing approval.
6. Measure 3-second retention, average watch time, saves, shares, profile visits, qualified inquiries and booked consultations. No PHI in artifacts.
