# Viral Hook Lab
For each audience angle produce five distinct hooks: curiosity, myth-bust, visual demonstration, relatable POV, and practitioner explanation. Draft 15-30 second vertical storyboard with 0-2s hook, 3-18s proof/explanation, 19-25s payoff, and optional final CTA. Use actual footage and staff consent. Never invent clinical outcomes, imply guaranteed results, shame appearances, or promise virality. Score hooks for specificity, clarity, surprise, relevance and policy safety. Test at most two hooks per concept, varying only the opening when possible. Clinical topics require Sentinel and provider signoff before publishing.
