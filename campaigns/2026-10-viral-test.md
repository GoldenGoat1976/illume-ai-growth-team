# October 2026 Illumé viral creative test
Status: Draft for human and provider review. No posts published.

## Research signals
- Perimenopause-related skin changes are a current beauty editorial topic; don't present all changes as universal.
- Short 15-30s educational and treatment-process videos are promising formats, not guaranteed viral.
- The Menopause Society October 20, 2026 press release describes research on hormone therapy and GLP-1 outcomes; association is not proof of causality and provider approval is mandatory.

## Creative A: HydraFacial satisfaction
Audience: core skincare and adjacent self-care.
Hook A: 'What actually happens during a HydraFacial?'
Hook B: 'The most satisfying 15 seconds in our treatment room.'
Storyboard: 0-2s real treatment close-up; 3-12s cleanse/extraction/hydration footage; 13-22s practitioner explains steps; 23-28s 'See if a HydraFacial is right for you' CTA.
Caption: 'Curious what a HydraFacial appointment looks like? Here's a closer look at the process at Illumé in Lee's Summit. Tap the link in this post to book your HydraFacial.'
Metrics: 3s hold, completion, saves, profile clicks, bookings.

## Creative B: Skin after 40
Audience: perimenopause curiosity and skin-care.
Hook A: 'Your skincare may not be the problem. Your skin's needs can change.'
Hook B: 'Three skin changes we talk about with clients in midlife.'
Storyboard: 0-2s practitioner hook; 3-18s dryness, sensitivity, texture (not diagnoses); 19-25s personalized care approach; CTA consult.
Caption: 'Skin can change during midlife, and routines sometimes need to change too. Our team can help you explore personalized skincare and aesthetic options.'
Metrics: saves, qualified consults, completion.

## Creative C: Menopause and weight management (provider-review hold)
Audience: educational HRT and metabolic wellness.
Hook A: 'New menopause research is raising questions about weight management.'
Hook B: 'What can a new study tell us—and what can't it tell us?'
Storyboard: clinician introduces preliminary conference finding; explains association vs causation; encourages individualized medical discussion.
Caption: 'Emerging research is exploring the relationship between hormone therapy and weight-management treatment. One study cannot determine what is appropriate for an individual. Speak with a qualified clinician.'
Never claim combined therapy causes greater weight loss, prescribe a drug, or promise results.

## Test design
Post creative A first, B second after creative approval; hold C until provider signoff. Test two hooks on separate edits with consistent footage and captions. Record baseline, 24h and 72h metrics. Score qualified local consults higher than views. No automated publishing, patient data, paid spend, or medical advice in comments.

Audience Bullseye -> Spark Hook Lab -> Muse -> Sentinel -> Conductor human approval -> post manually -> Ledger measurement -> Context Strategy Audit.
